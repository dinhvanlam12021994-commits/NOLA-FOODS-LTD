Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

NOLA FOODS LTD respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared, and protected when you use our mobile games, applications, websites, and related digital services.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the game or application, we may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, application usage information, diagnostics, crash reports, analytics information, and advertising identifiers where applicable.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and maintain our games, improve performance, understand gameplay and usage trends, resolve technical issues, prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve the player experience.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our websites or third-party services used by our games may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, device identifiers, and similar technologies for functionality, analytics, security, or advertising. Where required by applicable law, consent will be requested before non-essential tracking technologies are used.

Third-Party Services

Our games may use third-party services for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, hosting, authentication, or other functionality. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Advertising

Some of our games may display advertisements. Advertising partners may use device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, measure, personalize, or limit advertising, subject to applicable law and your choices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our games, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

International Data Transfers

Information may be processed or stored in countries outside your country of residence. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to operate our games and services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or satisfy legal obligations.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can also manage certain app permissions and advertising choices through your device or application settings.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date where appropriate.

Contact Us

For privacy or game support questions, please use the developer contact details listed on the relevant Google Play app page.
